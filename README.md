## Professional Perspective

Before focusing heavily on AI and software, much of my career was built around operating systems in the non-software sense: procedures, reporting, quality controls, people, vendors, production, safety, purchasing, documentation, and making sure work actually moved from one step to the next.

That background shapes the way I build technology. I tend to think in terms of:

- What problem is the user actually trying to solve?
- What information has to persist?
- What can be automated safely?
- Where does human judgment still belong?
- What happens when the system fails?
- How will someone know what is waiting, blocked, completed, or wrong?
- Can another person understand and maintain the process later?
